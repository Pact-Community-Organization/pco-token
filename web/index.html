PCO Community Token

Free & valueless by design. PCO is a community experiment: an advisory governance token for Pact Community Organization tooling direction. It carries no dividends, no revenue rights, no monetary value, and is never sold. Votes are advisory signals — they execute nothing.

Advisory governance

Governance questions are ranked-choice: each question carries 2–5 named options and a ballot is your ranking of them, scored with Borda counts plus a head-to-head matrix. Questions are authored by the organisation from suggestions raised on the public channels — holding PCO does not open one, and there is no balance threshold of any kind.

Read the open questions and cast a ranked ballot on the PCO site: pact-community.org/token.

Votes are advisory signals — they execute nothing.
