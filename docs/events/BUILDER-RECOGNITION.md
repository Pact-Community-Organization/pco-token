# Builder Recognition — retroactive grants

Recognition for **work already done** for the Pact/Kadena community, without anyone having
asked. Program context: [EVENT-PROGRAM.md](EVENT-PROGRAM.md).

## What qualifies (batch 1 criteria)

Work that was **public, shipped, and load-bearing for the post-2025 community** — for example:

- maintaining or materially contributing to the community-edition node, language, or client
  libraries;
- building and maintaining tools, wallets, explorers, or infrastructure the community actually
  uses;
- publishing substantial educational material (docs, tutorials, reference contracts) that
  developers rely on;
- keeping community venues alive and useful through the transition.

Not eligible: holding tokens, social-media activity, being early, or work that was already
paid for at market rates by a company.

## Process (each batch)

1. **Criteria + window announced** on all channels before any selection.
2. Judges (named humans) collect candidates from public evidence — repositories, releases,
   published material. **The work does not apply to us; we look for the work.** Anyone may
   point out work we missed, in public, during the window.
3. Conflict-of-interest rule: a judge with any personal stake in a candidacy says so publicly
   and abstains on it.
4. Grants land on-chain with a public reason naming the work (`AWARDED` events, individual
   grants up to 2,000 PCO; batch 1 up to 25,000 PCO total, later batches up to 10,000 PCO
   quarterly).
5. The batch announcement lists every recipient, the reason, and the on-chain reference. If
   someone declines, we say so and return the amount to the pool's headroom.

## Why this shape

Retroactive, human-judged recognition of named contributors is the one distribution pattern
with measured positive contributor retention — and it is immune to farming, because the only
way to "game" it is to have already done real work in public.
